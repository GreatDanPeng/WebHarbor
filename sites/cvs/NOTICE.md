# CVS source material notice

This independent WebHarbor research mirror is not affiliated with or endorsed by CVS Health, CVS Pharmacy, or the product manufacturers. The public-source snapshot was captured on September 28, 2026. CVS names, trademarks, campaign photographs, product images, descriptions, store information, and policy text remain the property of their respective owners. No license to those third-party materials is claimed by this contribution.

Source URLs, capture timestamps, evidence hashes, limitations, and image hashes are recorded in `provenance.json` and `asset_inventory.json`. The image archive contains only observed CVS homepage and product image URLs. It includes no analytics, advertising pixels, third-party scripts, customer information, or original authentication data. These source materials are reproduced to evaluate web-navigation agents in an isolated research environment. Do not use the mirror as a live pharmacy, medical service, commerce service, or source of current pricing and availability.

The corpus contains 40 listing products across oral care and shampoo, two complete product details, 25 Illinois stores, and four help pages. The remaining product descriptions and unobserved variants are not invented. CVS returned Page Not Found for one detail and Access Denied on subsequent product navigation; those limits are retained in the provenance manifest. Three range-priced listings are not purchasable in the local demo without a captured exact variant price. Store and pharmacy schedules are separate and retain the captured dates.

User accounts, saved addresses, favorites, carts, and orders are explicitly synthetic local benchmark fixtures. No prescription actions, appointments, messages, real payment information, or external orders are processed. The checkout demonstration has no real tax calculation or fulfillment.

For attribution corrections or removal requests, open an issue at https://github.com/aiming-lab/WebHarbor with the source URL and affected asset path so maintainers can replace or remove the material from the code and dataset archives.
